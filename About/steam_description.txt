[h1]RimSynapse - Regions and Territories[/h1]
[b][url=https://github.com/RimSynapse/Regions-and-Territories/wiki]Official Wiki and Documentation[/url][/b]
[b]Overhauls the world map generation with dynamic geographic province generation, world map overlays, and territory borders.[/b]

---

[h2]⚠ Requires a New Colony - Not Save-Game Compatible[/h2]
[b]Install this before you generate a world, not partway through a playthrough.[/b]

Regions and Territories does most of its work during world generation. It draws the province boundaries and then places faction settlements according to them, respecting region occupancy limits, border buffers, and sequential expansion. Add it to a world that already exists and the provinces get drawn around settlements that were never placed by any of those rules - so territory ownership, occupancy, and border logic all report results that look broken, because the world they are describing never followed the rules they assume.

Removing it from an in-progress save will log errors about missing province data when that save is next loaded. The save will usually still open, but this is not a supported path and you should expect a wall of red text.

Regions and Territories divides the globe into dynamic geographic provinces with defined borders, population densities, custom road linkages, and procedurally placed ruins.

---

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Geographic Domain Mapping:[/b] Splits the world map into dynamic provinces with clear geographic boundaries.
[*] [b]Faction Territory Overlays:[/b] Displays visual, expandable territory control maps using the Map Mode Framework.
[*] [b]Procedural Trade Infrastructure:[/b] Dynamically links generated settlements with trade roads and places historical ruins on the world map.
[/list]
